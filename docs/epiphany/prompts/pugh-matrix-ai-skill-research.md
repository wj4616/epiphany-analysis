**Pugh Matrix Overview:**
A Pugh Matrix (decision matrix / Pugh chart) is a systematic decision-making tool that helps teams evaluate multiple alternatives against predefined criteria. It provides a structured approach for comparing different concepts, solutions, or options in engineering design, product development, and business decision-making processes.

**Origin:** Developed by Stuart Pugh, a British design engineer, who recognized the need for a systematic approach to concept selection. The methodology eliminates subjective bias and emotional decision-making by providing an objective framework that considers multiple evaluation criteria simultaneously.
